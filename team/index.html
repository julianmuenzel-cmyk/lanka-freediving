Your Instructor

Your instructor.

I don't just teach you to hold your breath. I teach you to find stillness.
Molchanovs Instructor Evolution Spearfishing Competition Safety Diver
[ photo: instructor portrait ]

Molchanovs certified instructor. Evolution Spearfishing instructor. Competition safety diver. Based in Unawatuna from November to April — maximum 4 students per course.

I teach freediving the way I dive: with precision, patience, and safety at the centre. Less about chasing depth, more about presence — the quiet before you descend, the rhythm of your breath, weightlessness in warm tropical water.

This isn't a dive shop. It's a school for people who want to learn something real — whether you're curious for an afternoon or ready for full certification.

Every student gets individual attention. I watch your technique in the pool before we ever touch open water. I adjust the pace to your body, your breath, your comfort — because rushing someone to depth is how accidents happen, and patience is how freedivers are made.

Season runs November — April. Based in Unawatuna, southern Sri Lanka.
