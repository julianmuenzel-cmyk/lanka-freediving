Our Approach

How we teach.

Three principles behind every course, every session, every descent.

Presence over depth

Less about chasing numbers, more about the quiet before you descend — the rhythm of your breath, weightlessness in warm tropical water.

Maximum 4 students

Safety first, always

Molchanovs standards, competition safety training, and conservative progression. Depth follows technique — never the other way around.

Unawatuna

Warm water. Clear visibility. Real marine life.

Based on the southern coast of Sri Lanka, Unawatuna offers some of the best freediving conditions in the Indian Ocean. Water temperatures sit between 27–30°C year-round during season — no wetsuit required for most divers.

Visibility regularly exceeds 15 metres on calm mornings. Reef walls, sandy bottoms, and open blue water within a short boat ride. Turtles, reef fish, and occasional whale sightings are part of the daily routine.

We operate November to April, when the southwest monsoon clears and the coast settles into its calmest, warmest months. Outside season, the school is closed — we dive when conditions are right.
