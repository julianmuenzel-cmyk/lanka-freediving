Molchanovs Wave 1 certification

Wave 1 — Beginner Freediving

Duration3 days
Price€350
Max students4

What you'll learn

  • Online theory and exam
  • Pool: static apnea (1:30), dynamic bi-fins (30 m), intro to no-fins, buddying and rescue
  • Open water: duck dive, Frenzel equalization, Free Immersion and Constant Weight to 12–20 m

Complete beginners ready for real certification. Snorkellers, scuba divers, swimmers comfortable in the ocean. Must swim 200 m unassisted.
