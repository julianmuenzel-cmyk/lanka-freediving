The schedule

A week in the water

Five days of progressive training — from arrival and breathwork to certification and celebration.

Day 1

Arrival · Breathwork · Shallow pool

Welcome briefing, pranayama and relaxation fundamentals, first shallow pool session to find your rhythm in the water.

Day 2

Pool · STA & DYN

Static apnea and dynamic training in the pool. Buddying, rescue drills, and building confidence before the ocean.

Day 3

First open water

Boat out at dawn. Duck dives, line work, and your first proper open water freedives in warm tropical conditions.

Day 4

Depth training

Progressive depth sessions — freefall, Frenzel refinement, and technique work tailored to your level and goals.

Day 5

Certification · Celebration

Final dives, certification assessment, and a closing session to integrate everything you've learned. Dinner together.
