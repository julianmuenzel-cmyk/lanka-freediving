Multi-day trips for certified freedivers — Batanta Island, West Papua, and seasonal Sri Lanka reefs.

Batanta Island

Hunt at the edge of the world.

Once a year we take a small group of graduates to Batanta, West Papua, with Salty Tracks (Evolution Spearfishing). Eight days. Maximum 6 guests.

  • Daily spearfishing on Batanta reefs
  • Freediving depth training beside the hunting
  • Remote camping — Fam & Gam Islands
  • Manta cleaning station off the homestay dock
  • Fresh catch dinners — conservation-first

Base: Batanta Adventure Homestay. Wave 2 or equivalent. Intermediate spearfishing preferred. From $2,200, meals and lodging included.
