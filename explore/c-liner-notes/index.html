Your instructor

“I don't just teach you to hold your breath. I teach you to find stillness.”

Julian is a Molchanovs instructor, an Evolution Spearfishing instructor, and a certified safety diver. He learned to freedive in Lake Zurich, then in Zanzibar, the Blue Hole in Egypt, and the Pacific off Costa Rica, where he co-founded Evolution Spearfishing. He teaches with precision and patience — less about chasing depth, more about what happens in the quiet before you descend.

Courses

Three ways in. A half-day to see if the water agrees with you, then two Molchanovs certifications if it does.

The list
Course Certification Time Price For
Discover Freediving No certification Half day €150 Breathing and relaxation fundamentals, a shallow-water breath-hold session, safety basics. For snorkellers, surfers, and the curious.
Wave 1 Molchanovs Wave 1 certification 3 days €350 Online theory, pool work, static and dynamic apnea, Frenzel equalization, open water to 12–20 m. For complete beginners ready for real certification.
Wave 2 Molchanovs Wave 2 certification 4 days €450 Mouthfill equalization, free immersion, rescue skills, depth to 24–30 m. For certified freedivers going deeper.

The process

Getting here is simple. Write first, meet on land, then go out on the water.

  1. Get in touch

    Message us with your dates. We'll confirm within 24 hours and send theory access.

  2. Meet at the hotel

    Gear provided, briefing, pool session. Get comfortable before the ocean.

  3. Ocean

    Boat out early, open water dives, certification. Photos included.
