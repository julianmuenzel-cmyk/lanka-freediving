How we teach

Presence over depth

Less chasing numbers. More the quiet before you descend.

Maximum 4 students

Every breath and every question gets time. Not a conveyor belt.

Safety first

Molchanovs standards, competition safety training, conservative progression.

Unawatuna

Warm water. Clear mornings.

Southern Sri Lanka. Water 27–30°C in season. Visibility often over 15 m on calm mornings. Reef, sand, and blue water a short boat ride out. Turtles are ordinary. We run November to April, then we close.
