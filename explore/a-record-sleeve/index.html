At a glance

Lanka Freediving is a Molchanovs-certified freediving school in Unawatuna, Sri Lanka, running Discover, Wave 1, and Wave 2 courses in groups of four, November through April.

  • Location: Unawatuna, southern Sri Lanka
  • Beginner option: Discover Freediving, no experience required
  • Gear provided on all courses
  • We reply within 24 hours
Can complete beginners learn freediving in Sri Lanka?

Yes — when taught properly. We follow the Molchanovs safety framework: never dive alone, always with a trained buddy, progressive depth. We start in the pool. Your instructor is a Molchanovs instructor and a competition safety diver.

Which freediving course should I choose?

Short on time: Discover. Ready to commit: Wave 1. Already certified: we'll place you in Wave 2 or coaching.

When is the freediving season in Unawatuna?

November through April. We don't run courses outside that window.
