Side A · Unawatuna

Your instructor

“I don't just teach you to hold your breath. I teach you to find stillness.”

Julian is a Molchanovs instructor, an Evolution Spearfishing instructor, and a certified safety diver. He learned to freedive in Lake Zurich, then in Zanzibar, the Blue Hole in Egypt, and the Pacific off Costa Rica, where he co-founded Evolution Spearfishing. He teaches with precision and patience — less about chasing depth, more about what happens in the quiet before you descend.

Discover Freediving

No certification · Half day · €120

Breathing and relaxation fundamentals, a shallow-water breath-hold session, safety basics. For snorkellers, surfers, and the curious.

Wave 1

Molchanovs Wave 1 certification · 3 days · €350

Online theory, pool work, static and dynamic apnea, Frenzel equalization, open water to 12–20 m. For complete beginners ready for real certification.

33 rpm

 

Wave 2

Molchanovs Wave 2 certification · 4 days · €450

Mouthfill equalization, free immersion, rescue skills, depth to 24–30 m. For certified freedivers going deeper.
