Getting started

Yes — when taught properly. All our courses follow the Molchanovs safety framework: never dive alone, always with a trained buddy, and progressive depth limits based on your level. We start in shallow pool water before any open ocean diving. Your instructor is a certified Molchanovs instructor and competition safety diver.

For Wave 1 and above, you need to swim 200 metres unassisted — this is a Molchanovs certification requirement. For the Discover session, basic water comfort is enough. You don't need to be a competitive swimmer, but you should feel at ease floating and moving in water.

Moderate fitness is sufficient. Freediving is more about relaxation and breath control than athletic power. If you can walk up a few flights of stairs without difficulty and are generally healthy, you're likely fine. We'll discuss any medical conditions during booking — some conditions require a doctor's clearance.

Scuba uses compressed air and equipment to stay underwater for long periods. Freediving is a single breath — no tanks, no bubbles, no noise. It's quieter, more intimate with marine life, and focuses on breath awareness and efficiency rather than equipment management. Many scuba divers take freediving courses to improve their air consumption and comfort in the water.

Molchanovs is the world's leading freediving education system, founded by Alexey Molchanov — the deepest man in history. Their Wave courses (1, 2, 3) are internationally recognised and provide a structured progression from beginner to advanced. Certification is digital and valid worldwide at any Molchanovs school.

Courses

If you're short on time or unsure, start with Discover — a half-day introduction with no certification. If you're ready to commit, Wave 1 is the full beginner course over 3 days. Already certified elsewhere? We can assess your level and place you in Wave 2 or advanced training. Message us and we'll recommend the right fit.

Maximum 4 students per course — often fewer. This isn't a factory dive shop. Small groups mean more individual attention, safer ratios in the water, and sessions tailored to your pace. The retreat accepts up to 8 guests, but water sessions are still run in small groups.

Wave 1 and Wave 2 include online theory access, pool sessions, open water dives, boat trips, all freediving gear (mask, fins, wetsuit, weight belt), and underwater photos. Discover includes gear and pool access. Accommodation and meals are not included in course prices — but we can recommend hotels nearby.

Absolutely — many of our best students started exactly where you are. We never push you beyond your comfort zone. Depth is earned gradually through pool work first, then shallow open water, then deeper. Fear of depth often dissolves once you understand the physiology and have proper technique. Patience is part of the process.

Molchanovs certifications don't expire — once certified, you're certified for life. That said, if you haven't dived in a year or more, we recommend a refresher session before jumping back into open water. Skills fade faster than the certificate does.

Sri Lanka & logistics

Our season runs November through April, when the southwest monsoon keeps Unawatuna calm and clear. Water visibility is typically 10–20 metres, sometimes better. Outside this window we don't run courses — the ocean conditions on the south coast aren't suitable for beginner and intermediate training.

We're based in Unawatuna, on the south coast of Sri Lanka — about 15 minutes from Galle Fort and 2.5 hours from Colombo airport. Sessions start from a partner hotel on the beach. You'll receive the exact meeting point and time when you book. Open water dives are accessed by boat from Unawatuna Bay.

Swimwear, reef-safe sunscreen, a towel, and flip-flops. We provide all freediving gear. Bring any personal items you prefer — some students like their own mask if they have one that fits well. For the retreat, pack light tropical clothing and a reusable water bottle. A rash vest is useful but not essential.

Yes — certified divers can rent full freediving kit by the day or week. This includes long fins, wetsuit, weight belt, and lanyard. Let us know your sizes when booking so we can have everything ready. For courses, all gear is included in the price.

Yes — water temperature in season is typically 27–29°C. Most students are comfortable in a 3 mm wetsuit or even a rash vest for shorter sessions. We provide wetsuits as part of the course. The warm water is one of the best reasons to train in Sri Lanka — no cold shock, longer comfortable sessions.

Booking

Fill in the enquiry form on our website, message us on WhatsApp, or email hello@lankafreediving.com. Tell us your dates, experience level, and which course you're interested in. I'll confirm availability within 24 hours and send payment details. Online theory access starts as soon as you pay the deposit.

Yes — a 30% deposit secures your spot. The balance is due on the first day of your course. For the retreat and expedition, a 50% deposit is required due to limited spaces. Deposits can be paid by bank transfer or card — we'll send details when you book.

Cancel more than 14 days before your start date and your deposit is fully refundable. Within 14 days, the deposit can be transferred to a future date within the same season. No-shows forfeit the deposit. Weather cancellations on our end are always rescheduled or fully refunded — we never charge you for conditions we can't control.
