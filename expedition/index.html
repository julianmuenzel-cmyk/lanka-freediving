In Collaboration with Salty Tracks

Hunt at the edge of the world.

Once a year we take a small group of Lanka Freediving graduates to Batanta Island, West Papua — one of the last truly wild reef systems on earth. In partnership with Salty Tracks (by Evolution Spearfishing), this is a spearfishing and freediving expedition for people who want the real thing.

8 days. Maximum 6 guests. Hand-picked crew.

What to expect

  • Daily spearfishing on pristine Batanta reefs
  • Freediving depth training alongside the hunting sessions
  • Remote island camping — Fam & Gam Islands, off-grid
  • Manta cleaning station right off the homestay dock
  • Fresh catch dinners every night — you spear it, you eat it
  • Conservation-first: only what feeds the group and the local village

Base: Batanta Adventure Homestay, run by Hans — bungalows on the beach, coral reef at the doorstep. This isn't a resort. It's a base camp.

Requirements

Wave 2 certification or equivalent. Intermediate+ spearfishing experience preferred. All freediving gear provided.
